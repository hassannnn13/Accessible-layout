Chicken Biryani

Layers of rice, marinated chicken, vegetables and fried onion, built to feed exactly as many people as are actually coming.

Servings

Showing quantities for 1 serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups basmati rice, washed and soaked 30 minutes
  • 1 kg chicken, bone-in, cut into pieces
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced and fried until deep gold
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 4 green chillies, slit
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 tbsp biryani masala
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ cup oil or ghee
  • ¼ tsp saffron threads, soaked in 3 tbsp warm milk
  • 1 cup mint leaves, roughly chopped
  • ½ cup coriander leaves, roughly chopped
  • 4 cups water, for parboiling the rice
  • To taste salt

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken

    Mix the chicken with yogurt, half the ginger-garlic paste, red chilli powder, turmeric and salt. Cover and set aside for at least 30 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.

  2. Parboil the rice

    Bring the water to a boil with a little salt and a whole spice or two if you have them (bay leaf, cardamom, cinnamon). Add the soaked rice and cook until it is about 70% done — still firm at the centre. Drain and set aside.

  3. Cook the chicken masala

    Heat the oil or ghee. Add the remaining ginger-garlic paste and green chillies, then the tomatoes, and cook until soft. Add the marinated chicken and biryani masala, and cook uncovered until the chicken is tender and the masala has thickened around it.

  4. Layer the pot

    Spread the chicken masala across the base of a heavy pot. Layer the parboiled rice over it. Scatter over the fried onions, mint, and coriander. Drizzle the saffron milk over the top.

  5. Seal and steam

    Cover tightly — a layer of foil under the lid helps trap the steam. Cook on very low heat for 20 to 25 minutes, until the rice is fully done and the flavours have come together.

  6. Rest, then serve

    Turn off the heat and let the pot rest, still covered, for 10 minutes. Fluff gently from the bottom up so the layers mix, and serve hot with raita.
